Your Future Starts Today

Established in 1974 in Dipolog City, DMC College Foundation has evolved
from a hospital-based nursing and midwifery program into the province's leading
multidisciplinary institution. Now spanning a 22,500-square-meter campus under
LaSallian supervision, the college offers diverse courses ranging from healthcare
and IT to Law and Basic Education, serving as a vital educational landmark
for Zamboanga del Norte.
